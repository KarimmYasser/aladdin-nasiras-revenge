########### First Meeting

Genie:
Aladdin… I don’t have much time. Something went wrong.

Aladdin:
Genie? Where’s Jasmine?

Genie:
She’s been taken. Not by a thief… not by a guard… something older.

Aladdin:
Who took her?

Genie:
A being that feeds on wishes. He’s been hiding beyond the lamp’s magic.

Aladdin:
Why Jasmine?

Genie:
Because she matters to you. And your wishes are strong.

Aladdin:
Tell me where he is.

Genie:
I can’t see him clearly… but the closer you get, the stronger I become.


########### Second Meeting

Genie:
Listen carefully, this will help you survive.
The lamp is responding to danger… it’s unlocking a hidden trick.

Genie:
Press “P” and you’ll enter a special mode… call it an apple fight if you like.
In that state, you can throw apples at your enemies.

Genie:
They may look simple… but they hit much harder than anything else you have.
Use it when you’re overwhelmed… or when something refuses to fall.



########### Third Meeting

Genie:
You’re getting closer. I can feel him now.

Aladdin:
Then tell me everything.

Genie:
He’s called the Wish Eater. Every wish he takes makes him stronger.

Aladdin:
And Jasmine?

Genie:
He didn’t just capture her… he bound her to his power.

Aladdin:
Meaning?

Genie:
As long as he exists, she cannot escape.

Aladdin:
Then I destroy him.

Genie:
Be careful. He’ll try to break you… make you doubt your own wishes.





########### Fourth Meeting

Genie:
This is it. He’s waiting for you.

Aladdin:
I’m ready.

Genie:
Listen carefully… he will offer you a deal.

Aladdin:
What kind of deal?

Genie:
He’ll promise to return Jasmine… if you give up your final wish.

Aladdin:
And if I refuse?

Genie:
Then you’ll have to fight him at full strength… and risk losing everything.

Aladdin:
I’m not trading her life for a trick, I will fight him!!
